PIN tools

A ruse PIN looks and verifies like the real one, but every ballot cast with it is discarded at tally. Hand it over if you are being coerced.

Leave the box empty and the app draws one for you. Type a value to choose it yourself - the one you already gave someone, so that it keeps working after a re-send or a revocation. Whatever you type becomes the decoy, and this app will not tell you whether it matches your own PIN - an app that could tell you would also tell anyone testing PINs on your device. If you do type your own, nothing is lost: that decoy is your real credential, so its ballots still count, and arming any other value gives you a decoy again.

Arming a decoy replaces the credential this app checks your PIN against, so it is you who has to ask for it: type the PIN this app holds.

"Re-send my PIN" delivers YOUR PIN again. If someone is watching and you want them to see a PIN that is not yours, use the ruse PIN above instead. Both show a PIN the same way on this screen; on the network they differ, so someone watching your connection could tell them apart.

Revoke credential

Deletes your current voting identity: every ballot cast with it becomes void - including one you have already confirmed and seen counted - and you are issued a new pseudonymous id and a new PIN. Vote again afterwards.
